Hey, I'm Pepper

Network security technician by day, homelab tinkerer by night.

Chili Lab is my blog, and I'm Pepper, the person who writes every article on it. It covers homelab, networking, security and self-hosted AI agents, with the real configs and fixes I used.

About me

By day I work as a network security technician. At night I run a homelab on Proxmox and Docker, mostly DNS, VPNs and reverse proxies, plus a small team of AI agents that helps me look after it. I learn by building things and breaking them, then writing down how I got them working again. Right now I am studying for the Cisco CCNA.

Certifications

  • CompTIA Security+
  • Fortinet NSE 3
  • Microsoft AB-900
  • In progress: Cisco CCNA
